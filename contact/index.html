What happens after you get in touch

Four steps, the same whether you called or wrote.

  1. We call you back

    To ask a few questions about the tree and agree a time to come and see it.

  2. We walk the yard

    In person and free. We check the tree, the access, what is underneath and whose tree it is.

  3. You get a written quote

    Itemized, so you can compare it line by line. No obligation, and no pressure to decide on the spot.

  4. We book the work

    Permits and utility locates are arranged first where they apply. Then the job, the cleanup and a final walk with you.

When to call someone else first

  • Someone is hurt, or wires are sparking: 911.
  • A line is down, or a tree is on a line: MGE, 608-252-7000.
  • The tree is on the terrace, in a park or on a greenway: City of Madison Urban Forestry, 608-266-4816, or 608-255-2345 after hours.
